The article considers a methodological approach to the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools in the learning and research activities of a master's student, including the preparation of the final qualification work. Drawing on the activity approach and empirical studies of cognitive offloading, the paper argues that AI acts as a means rather than a subject of research and proposes a model for distributing functions between the student and AI with a mandatory verification loop. The student's tasks are grouped into nine classes; for each class, specific evaluation criteria are defined and leaders are identified both among all tools and among those available from Russia without restrictions. An experiment on deploying open-weight language models on a researcher's workstation without server infrastructure is described. The results can be used by master's students and supervisors when organizing work on a thesis.
